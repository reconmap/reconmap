Your language, theme and timezone are your preferences. To change them, open the user menu in the top bar, choose **Your profile**, select **Edit**, and find the **Preferences** section.

The timezone lets others see dates and times relative to where you live, and shows when you might be available to chat and discuss your projects.

![User preferences](/images/screenshots/user-preferences.png)

You can also choose the dark or light theme in this section.

![User preferences with light theme](/images/screenshots/user-preferences-light-theme.png)

Everyone can see these preferences, but only you can change them. Administrators can view them on the user's edit page but not change them.
