Comments are short notes that you can leave on a project, a task or a finding. Use them to share context with your team, such as the outcome of a call with the client or a reminder for the next retest. Comments support Markdown.

Anyone who can access a project, task or finding can read its comments. Comments are not included in generated reports.

## Adding a comment

1. Open the project, task or finding, then select the **Comments** tab.
2. Write your comment in the box.
3. Select **Add comment**.

Comments are listed newest first. Each one is shown as a card with its author and the time it was posted above the rendered Markdown. Clients can read comments but cannot add them.

## Deleting a comment

Select the trash icon next to a comment and confirm. You can delete your own comments. Administrators and superusers can delete any comment.
