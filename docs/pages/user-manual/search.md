As the name of this page suggests, search is the feature in Reconmap that allows you to find projects, tasks, vulnerabilities quickly by only typing part of their name. Type your search term in the search box at the top of the screen and hit enter to see the results.

Matching is case-insensitive and looks at the name of projects and project templates, and at the summary or description of tasks, vulnerabilities and vulnerability templates. Only the matching items are shown in each section; sections with no matches are hidden and listed as "No results were found".
